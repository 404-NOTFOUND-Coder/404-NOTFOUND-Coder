
# Hi there, I'm Rongsen Wu 👋

🎓 Researcher at the **University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)**.

🔬 My research focuses on **Computer Vision, Multimodal Learning, and Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence**, with particular interests in long-video understanding and vision-language reasoning.

### 🔍 Research Interests

- 🎥 **Long-Video Understanding** — Temporal reasoning, video question answering, and evidence acquisition.
- 🧠 **Vision-Language Models** — Multimodal reasoning and efficient inference.
- 🤖 **Multi-Agent Systems** — Collaborative reasoning and tool-augmented intelligence.
- 🔗 **Trustworthy AI** — Hallucination mitigation and knowledge-grounded generation.
- 🌐 **Video Scene Understanding** — Dynamic scene graphs and spatiotemporal representation learning.

### 🚀 Research Projects

- **TEACG** — Training-free long-range video question answering through temporal evidence acquisition and consensus-gated answer intervention.
- **KG-MTA** — Knowledge-graph-grounded multi-tool reasoning for trustworthy user profile generation.
- **Video Scene Graph Generation** — Spatiotemporal feature learning with global-local Transformer architectures.
